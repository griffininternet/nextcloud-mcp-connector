Access log excerpts of the hosted connector runs, plan 03-09.

Source: the Caddy access log of the staging instance https://nc-staging.infranode.dev
on 2026-08-16 (UTC). The instance was deleted right after this file was written, so
these lines are the archived evidence behind AUTH-04 and behind the tables in
docs/oauth-setup.md.

Format: time, method, status, path, duration, User-Agent (truncated).
Query strings are cut off on purpose and the Login Flow v2 tokens are shortened;
nothing here is a credential and nothing here is reusable. Lines of our own tooling
(curl, the flow check script) are left out, as are the HaRP lookups
(/index.php/apps/app_api/harp/user-info, exactly one per MCP request), whose count
is the Success Criterion 5 measurement in docs/oauth-setup.md.

grant screen, and this app's consent page. The connector never sees the password.
